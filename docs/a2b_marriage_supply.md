# A2b task 5 — marriage supply

Measured after regulation (seeds 0–29, 100 years): **share of adults 25+ who never marry** is typically **0–2%** via `chronicle.run --stats`.

Cross-house-only pairing is sufficient for most of the run; a **same-house fallback for singles aged 30+** avoids stranding when the eligible opposite-sex pool is exhausted in small kin groups. This is not courtship (C3): acceptance rules and prestige gap are unchanged.
